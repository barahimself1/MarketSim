# Model contract

All prices are integer cents and quantities integer lots. One engine represents one instrument. Limit orders cross opposing quotes at maker prices before resting their remainder. Market orders are IOC: fills are retained and any remaining quantity is cancelled. Rejected input raises a validation error before entering the order book; rejected orders are not persisted as order states.

Midprice = (best bid + best ask)/2. Microprice = (ask × bid size + bid × ask size)/(bid size + ask size). The imbalance displayed in replay is (top-ten bid depth − top-ten ask depth)/total top-ten depth. Missing sides make midprice/microprice unavailable.

The synthetic generator has six preset regimes. A cancellation chooses a known resting order. Market instructions match the current engine state. New liquidity is placed around the current mid or a fixed anchor when one side is absent. This is a mechanics fixture, not a calibrated agent-based market model. Event time is 100 ms per instruction. Quote statistics are event-weighted; effective spread is an equal-weight mean across individual fills against the pre-instruction mid, not a volume-weighted estimate.

Every parent scenario rebuilds its initial book from the same event prefix and receives the same future background instructions. Its own executions consume liquidity and alter future availability. Cancellations for orders already consumed by the parent are ignored. Background agents do not respond to the parent; this limits causal impact interpretation.

Market execution walks available levels once. Passive execution joins the back of the best same-side FIFO queue. No hidden liquidity or latency is assumed. TWAP allocates integer lots over ten deterministic child slots. VWAP uses a prior profile; uniform VWAP intentionally equals TWAP. Largest remainders conserve total scheduled quantity. A child that cannot fully execute is IOC, and later schedules do not retry its remainder. POV accumulates fractional credits from the previous event's external executed volume and places integer child quantities one event later. The displayed POV percentage is a fraction of background volume, not a strict cap on total market participation.

Cost sign is +1 for buys and −1 for sells. Slippage is signed (average fill − arrival mid)/arrival mid in basis points. Total implementation shortfall is filled cost + fees + unfilled opportunity, divided by arrival mid × requested quantity. Opportunity uses the terminal mid at the common horizon. A missing terminal mid yields unavailable total shortfall only when quantity remains unfilled; a completed order has zero opportunity cost. Missing contemporaneous midpoints make spread/walk and delay components unavailable, rather than zero. Completion time is reported only for fully filled parents; incomplete orders have null completion time and a recorded horizon.

Filled cost decomposes exactly into signed fill-versus-current-mid and signed current-mid-versus-arrival terms. Spread crossing and book walking are combined; neither is presented as identified permanent market impact. The same formula handles passive maker fills. Fees are configured uniformly, with no assumed maker rebate. There is no independent earlier decision price, so delay before arrival is not measured.

Experiment seeds are independent fixture episodes, while strategies within a seed share background instructions. Distributions report mean, median, population standard deviation, nearest-rank empirical P95 and full-completion rate. The app does not perform hypothesis tests or automatically assign SUPPORTED status. All records remain SIMULATION ONLY. No cherry-picked seed is promoted as evidence of historical strategy superiority.

Replay indices are zero-based while event sequence numbers are one-based. Scheduling uses event-index slots, appropriate to this uniformly spaced synthetic fixture. Completion and observed horizon durations are timestamp differences. An N-event interval spans N−1 observed inter-event gaps; no time beyond the last processed event is observed. Historical irregular-time TWAP requires a timestamp-based scheduler.

Browser experiment records persist in local storage and can be exported. This is not a shared server registry. The bundled `data/experiment-deep.json` preserves a measured 20-seed run, full settings and per-episode cost/completion observations. Missing costs are counted explicitly; empirical P95 with 20 episodes is not a reliable tail-risk estimate.
